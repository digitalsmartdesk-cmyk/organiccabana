Our story

From the field to your chakki, with nothing in between.

At Medhavat, we believe the wisdom of good food has always been simple: choose the right grain, mill it with patience, and add nothing else. Our stone mills run slow and cold — preserving the natural oils, bran and germ that industrial roller mills routinely strip away in the name of shelf life.

We source from Indian farms, mill in small batches, and seal our flours while they're still fresh. Every pack carries what it promises — and only what it promises. No maida blends, no artificial colours, no preservatives. Just wholesome flour, the way it was always meant to be made.

The Medhavat promise

Why we mill differently

Three principles that guide every grain we source, every batch we mill, and every pack we seal.

01

Stone-milled for maximum goodness

Cold-press stone milling runs at low speed, generating minimal heat. The result: natural oils, fibre and germ are preserved intact — the nutritional profile that high-speed roller mills routinely destroy to make flour last longer on a shelf.

02

Ancient grains, modern wisdom

Eleven products spanning wheat, millets and legumes — each grain chosen for its nutritional profile. Ragi for calcium, jowar for fibre, bajra for iron, besan for protein. Geography and tradition guide every recipe; we just mill carefully and stay out of the way.

03

Nothing hidden, nothing added

No artificial colours, flavours or preservatives — ever. No maida blending in wheat atta. No anti-caking agents. What you read on the label is the complete ingredient list. We print it in large type because we have nothing to hide.
