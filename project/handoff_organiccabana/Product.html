About this product

The real taste of whole wheat.

Grown without pesticides, milled without compromise. This is atta the way it was always meant to be.

Organic Cabana Whole Wheat Atta begins with select organic wheat grains sourced from certified farms where no synthetic pesticides or chemical fertilisers have touched the soil. Every grain is verified before it enters our stone mills.

Our cold-press stone mills run slow, generating minimal heat. The result is flour that retains its natural wheat germ oil, bran, and endosperm intact — the nutritional profile that high-speed industrial roller mills routinely destroy to extend shelf life on supermarket shelves.

One ingredient. Organic whole wheat. Nothing else — no additives, no anti-caking agents, no maida blending, no preservatives. Our ingredient list is the shortest in the category because we have nothing to hide.
