About this product

The real taste of whole wheat.

Our atta is stone-ground in small batches from single-origin wheat, preserving every trace of nutrition that modern roller mills strip away. This is the flour your grandmother would recognise.

Organic Cabana Whole Wheat Atta is milled from hand-picked, certified-organic wheat grown in the fertile black soil of Madhya Pradesh. Every grain is sun-dried and cleaned before being slowly stone-ground at low temperatures to retain the natural oils, fibre and micronutrients locked inside the bran and germ.

The result is a coarse, fragrant flour that makes rotis soft yet sturdy, with that unmistakable nutty sweetness that only fresh-milled whole wheat can deliver. Unlike refined or blended flours, our atta contains zero maida, zero additives and zero preservatives — just pure wheat, nothing else.

Packed within hours of milling in a nitrogen-flushed, food-grade pouch to lock in freshness, every bag carries full traceability back to the farm it came from. Whether you are making chapatis, parathas, puris or pizza bases, this atta gives you consistently soft results with the full nutritional benefit of whole grain.
